# Etsy listings — paste-ready

Three made-to-order digital listings. Everything below is written to Etsy's field limits
(title ≤ 140 chars, 13 tags ≤ 20 chars each, personalization prompt ≤ 256 chars) and to
Etsy's Creativity Standards: the description states plainly that the narrator is a
synthetic (AI) voice, that the art is original hand-drawn vector work, and that the music
is procedurally arranged from public-domain melodies. Do not soften those lines.

Once the listings are live, paste their URLs into `LINKS` at the top of
`site/shop/index.html` (`etsyBirthday`, `etsyGoodnight`, `etsyStory`).

| Listing photos | 1. product poster (1280×720 becomes 2000×1125 for Etsy: export the thumbnail at 2000 wide) 2. three stills from the video 3. a "how personalization works" card 4. the file-format / delivery card 5. a "what parents say" card once you have reviews. Etsy crops the thumbnail to 4:3 in search: keep the name centred |

| Production partner | none |

Etsy's AI disclosure: in the listing's **description** (mandatory) and in **About → shop policies**, keep the sentence
"The narrator is a synthetic voice (AI text-to-speech) that we run on our own computer" verbatim. Etsy asks that
sellers are honest about how items are made, and a synthetic voice is the kind of thing buyers file complaints about
when they discover it afterwards, not before.

## Order-handling checklist (per order)

1. Read the personalization box; if the pronunciation is ambiguous, message the buyer before rendering.
2. Render with the personalization flags (see `HANDOFF.md` and `bin/babyloop.mjs episode --help`); render at full quality, not `--preview`.
3. Listen to the name in the actual MP4. Check the title card spelling against the order.
4. Upload the MP4 (and MP3 / PNG where promised) to the delivery host; copy the link.
5. Etsy → Orders → the order → **Complete order** and paste `store/delivery-message.md` with the link. Marking it complete is what stops Etsy's processing-time clock.
6. Note the render seed and spec in the order notes, so a re-render is identical.
7. Delete the buyer's personalization text from your local queue after 30 days.
