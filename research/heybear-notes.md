**Who makes it.** Wayne and Lucie Johnson, UK; Wayne animates, Lucie produces. Hey Bear Productions Ltd incorporated 11 Apr 2019. The channel says all work is made by "Wayne and our team" with no generative AI. Romper (Feb 2022) reported 4–5 months per video and ~40 test renders per 30-second segment.

**Character roster.** Bear (mascot), the Dancing Fruit (Pineapple, Avocadosaurus, strawberries, cherries, Lemon added 2026), the Funky Veggies (Broccoli and others), Mindful Moon, the Sleepy Stars, Bizzy the bee (2026). The channel description claims Hey Bear Sensory® and Mindful Moon® as registered marks and Avocadosaurus as theirs.

**How a typical video is structured.** Verified: dance videos run 10–27 min; "Mini" versions 2–4 min; Shorts under 60 s; bedtime and classical videos 17 min to 2 h 12 min. Their vhx.tv page for the 27-min Disco Fruit Party credits six music tracks, so a segment is about 4–5 min, one track per scene, with a new cast or palette per track. Segment reuse is explicit in titles ("EXTENDED", "Best of", "1 Hour Mix") and in the 30-second build blocks Romper describes. Music is house, disco, hip-hop and cumbia per the founders; Screenwise calls it lo-fi and chill-hop. The six credited artists (Jimmy Fontanez, Slynk, Bird Creek, The Green Orbs, The Mini Vandals, Vibe Tracks) are YouTube Audio Library contributors, so the music appears to be licensed library tracks, not originals. No dialogue, by design, for multilingual reach. Unverified (video files not viewed): intro sting, exact cut frequency, transitions, BPM. Screenwise describes flat bright colour fields with dancing shapes and says dance segments run faster than Mindful Moon segments.

**Titles.** Long-form: `Hey Bear Sensory - {Theme}! - {format descriptor} - {music/purpose descriptor}`, e.g. "Hey Bear Sensory - Fruit Salad Dance Party - Counting 1 to 10 - Fun animation with music". Bedtime: "... - Mindful Moon and Sleepy Stars - 30 minutes - Bedtime Video". Shorts: brand plus theme plus 3–5 hashtags (#dancingfruit #animation #dancevideo). Recurring descriptors: "High Contrast Animation", "Visual Tracking", "Relaxing Video", "Sleep Video", "Classical Music".

**Descriptions.** YouTube served a bot wall, so exact text is unverified. The channel states that track titles are listed in descriptions; the channel description promotes their app (iOS, Apple TV, Roku, Android TV, Fire TV), store, plush, and a Macmillan book.

**Playlists (22)** are organised by series (Dancing Fruit, Funky Veggies, Mindful Moon and Sleepy Stars), by purpose (Relax, 30 Mins of Calm, 30 Min Bedtime Collection, Black/White/Red, High Contrast Collection, Classical Music Selection), by format (Minis!, Shorts) and by season (Halloween, Summer '26).

**Posting history.** Oldest visible upload "High Contrast Animation" (14:12, 28M views) is about 14 years old (~2012); 2012–2018 output was black/white/red patterns, rainbow scenes and classical sleep videos at 3–5 uploads a year. Dancing Fruit started 2019 (Disco Fruit Party, ~7 years old, 237M). The four biggest videos (Smoothie Mix 379M, Fruit Salad 328M, Funky Veggies Extended 276M, Disco Fruit 237M) were all posted 2019–2020. Subscriber milestones: 100K Jun 2019; 750K Feb 2022; 1M May 2022; 2M Sep 2023; ~2.9M early 2025 (Licensing International, undated); 3.82M Apr 2026 (HypeAuditor snippet); 3.94M Sep 2026. Cadence rose to ~11 uploads in the last 12 months, including a Save the Children UK collaboration.

**Learn from vs. copy.** Legitimate to learn: a small recurring cast; series names mapped to a viewer state (play vs. wind-down); loops recombined into Minis, 10–27 min episodes and hour mixes; one track per scene; adult-tolerable music instead of nursery-rhyme vocals; no dialogue; deep playlists by purpose and length; roughly monthly long-form output. Copying: the characters (Avocadosaurus, Pineapple, Mindful Moon, Sleepy Stars), the names "Hey Bear" and "Dancing Fruit", their music, palettes and fruit-in-costume look. "Little Hues – Dancing Fruit Baby Sensory" (103K subs, 254M views since May 2024) shows imitation gets views and marks exactly the position to avoid.
