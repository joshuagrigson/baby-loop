Why parents trust it

Every character hand-drawn. Every note arranged from public-domain melodies. No ads, no autoplay, no data collected about your child.

Slow on purpose

No cut is faster than the music loop it sits on. Scenes drift; they never flash. The genre's overstimulation problem is real, so we built the opposite.

Moderate loudness

Mixed around −18 dBFS and normalised to broadcast −16 LUFS, so it's never louder than the show before it. Lullabies fade out; nothing autoplays next.

Made to watch together

Best enjoyed on your lap, with you naming the fruit and singing along. For under-twos, paediatric guidance favours co-viewing and short sessions, and so do we.

Nothing to track

No app, no account, no analytics on this page. The only thing we ever know about your child is the name you type, and we delete it from our queue after 30 days.

Questions

The things parents ask

What do you mean by "synthetic voice"?

The narrator is a neural text-to-speech voice that runs on our own computer, trained only on public-domain recordings. There is no human voice actor. We say this plainly on every listing because we think you should know before you buy, and because Etsy's creativity standards ask sellers to be transparent about AI tools.

What the voice says is written by us, one line at a time. We listen to every render before it's sent, especially the name, and re-render free if it isn't right.

Is it safe for a baby to watch?

It is calm by design: slow scene changes, no strobing, moderate loudness and soft endings. We still recommend watching together and keeping sessions short, in line with paediatric guidance for children under two. Nothing here makes developmental promises; it's a gentle video with nice songs in it.

What file do I get, and how?

A 1080p MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio) that plays on iPhones, Android, tablets, smart TVs, Plex and any laptop. The goodnight video also comes with an MP3. You receive a download link by Etsy message (or email, if you bought direct) within 24 hours of ordering. Links stay live for 30 days, so save a copy.

How do I make sure the name is said correctly?

There's a "how to say it" line in the personalisation box: write it the way you'd explain it to a friend ("Siobhan = shi-VAWN"). We check it by ear before sending. If it still isn't right, reply and we'll re-render it at no charge.

What's your refund policy?

Change your mind before we start rendering and you get a full refund. Once your video has been made and sent, it's yours; if anything is wrong with it (name, file, spelling) we fix it or refund you, your choice. Etsy's own buyer protection applies to Etsy orders.

Can I use it however I like?

Every purchase comes with a personal-use licence: watch it at home, play it at the party, share it with grandparents, put it on a USB stick for the TV. It doesn't cover reselling, uploading to YouTube or other platforms, or use in a daycare or business. For that, see the creator licence.

Do you collect any data about my child?

Only what you type in the personalisation box, which we use to make the video and delete from our render queue after 30 days. This page has no analytics or tracking, and the videos are plain files: nothing in them phones home.

Can I choose the songs or characters?

Yes. Mention it in the personalisation box. The whole catalogue is public-domain melodies (Twinkle, Brahms' Lullaby, Frère Jacques, a couple of dozen child-arranged classical themes) and a cast of 49 hand-drawn characters: fruit, farm and forest animals, sea creatures, garden bugs and night-time friends.
